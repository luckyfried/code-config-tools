# The fixture's photographs

`make_person.py` makes every picture of Maren's year from these photographs: each pillar's posts, stories and reels
from that pillar's files, graded toward the color the post leads with; the liked images from the coast files, graded
toward the sea colors; the five posts outside her system from the outlier files as they are. Each one is CC0, copied
from another kit's fixture in this repository at 1350 px on the long side at most.
