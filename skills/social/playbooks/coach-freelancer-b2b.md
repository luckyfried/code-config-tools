# Playbook: Coach, freelancer, B2B
Coaches, consultants, freelancers, agencies, accountants, B2B services, SaaS founders, podcasters.

## What actually works
- One opinion with a reason, from their own work: "Most pricing pages hide the one number buyers want."
- A specific lesson with a number in it: "We cut a client's onboarding from 9 emails to 3. Replies doubled." (only numbers they gave)
- Teardowns: a before/after of a real artifact (a headline, an invoice, a deck slide), with permission or their own.
- Lists that save someone time: "4 lines I delete from every proposal."
- The person's own face, if supplied, on statement posts. LinkedIn rewards a recognisable person over a logo.
- Plain text-first images: one strong sentence, set large, on a calm background.

## Post mix
A week is 3-5 posts on LinkedIn. A single "make my post" ask is one image plus the caption.

| Post type | Share | Study (library examples, inspiration only) | Image shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion / statement | 1-2 | clean_modern/statement (li_post) | the one sentence, type-only; or hero with their headshot |
| Lesson list | 1 | clean_modern/list (li_post) | 4 short lines, type-only |
| Teardown / framework | 1 | clean_modern/carousel-cover + inner + cta (ig_carousel slides, exported as a PDF-style set) | 5-8 slides, one point each |
| Client result / quote | 0-1 | clean_modern/quote (li_post) | a quote they supplied, name and role as given |
| Event / webinar / podcast episode | 0-1 | clean_modern/event (li_post) | title, date with computed weekday, time and zone |

## Rhythm
- LinkedIn: Tue, Wed, Thu at 7:30-8:30am local; a second slot 12:00pm. 3 posts a week beats 7.
- Carousels Tue or Wed morning; the opinion post Thu; a short personal/lesson post Fri if 4-5 posts.
- Reply to comments for the first hour; the caption should end on a real question only if they want answers.
- Instagram/X only if named: X gets the statement as `x_post` 1600x900, one sentence.
- Series ideas: "Teardown Tuesday", "One number" (a weekly metric), "Proposal notes".

## Hooks that work
- "I rewrote 40 pricing pages this year. The same line fails on almost all of them."
- "Your proposal is 11 pages. The client reads page 1 and the price."
- "We stopped sending decks before calls. Meetings got shorter and closed faster."
- "4 lines I delete from every client email."
- "Here's the invoice template I've used for 6 years. Steal it."
- "A client asked why their ads stopped working. It was the landing page headline."
- "Most onboarding emails ask for the same thing three times."
- "New episode: Dana Ortiz on hiring your first ops person. Out Thursday."

## Style family
- Default `clean_modern`. Pick `editorial` for writers, photographers, designers and anyone with a strong personal photo. Pick `bold_type` for loud opinion posts or a sales/marketing coach with a high-energy brand.
- Palette from their logo or site; otherwise off-white, near-black ink and one accent (signal orange, forest green, cobalt, mustard). Never purple, never the blue-to-purple gradient, never navy+teal+coral.
- Imagery: their headshot or workspace photo if supplied. Otherwise type-only. A CC stand-in may show a real object from the topic (an invoice on a desk, a whiteboard), never a stock team or handshake.

## Pitfalls
- Guru clichés: "6-figure", "7-figure", "crush it", "game-changer", "level up", "scale to the moon", "hustle", "unlock", "10x".
- Rocket ships, light bulbs, chess pieces, mountain summits, handshakes, laptop-on-a-beach.
- Broetry: one sentence per line for the whole post. Write paragraphs of 1-3 sentences.
- Engagement bait: "Agree?", "Thoughts?", "Comment YES for the template".
- Invented results, client names, revenue or "worked with 500+ founders".
- Text-heavy carousels: max one headline and 180 characters per slide.
- A "Book a call" button on the image. Say "link in the comments" or "DM me 'audit'".
- A two-image comparison on `li_post`: design it as a carousel or two statement slides.

## Defaults when the user gave nothing
- Platforms: LinkedIn only. Add Instagram or X only if named.
- Sizes: `li_post` 1200x1200 for single images; carousels as `ig_carousel` 1080x1350 slides; `x_post` 1600x900 if X is in scope; `link_card` 1200x630 for a newsletter or episode link.
- "A post": 1 `li_post` image + caption (no Story or MP4 unless Instagram is named). "A week": 3-4 posts incl. 1 carousel.
- Assumptions to state: LinkedIn only; type-only because no headshot; numbers used only as given; CTA is [link in comments]; first-person voice in their name.
