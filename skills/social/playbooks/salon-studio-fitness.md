# Playbook: Salon, studio and fitness
Hair salons, barbers, nail and lash studios, spas, yoga, pilates, gyms, trainers, dance studios.

## What actually works
- Real work on real clients: the fade from the side, the balayage in daylight, the nail set on a hand. Close, sharp, natural light.
- Before/after, only from the owner's own photos with the client's consent. Same angle, same light, side by side.
- The schedule as a post: "This week: Mon 6am Flow, Wed 6pm Strength, Sat 9am Beginners". People screenshot it.
- Open slots: "Two chairs open Thursday after 3." Specific scarcity books appointments.
- The person doing the work, named only if given: the stylist's chair, the instructor mid-cue.
- First-visit offers with plain terms: "First class $10", "New clients: $15 off a cut".

## Post mix
A week is 6 posts. A single "make my post" ask is one hero plus its Story.

| Post type | Share | Study (library examples, inspiration only) | Image shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| The work | 2 | quiet_luxury/hero (salon) or bold_type/hero (gym) | their photo of a finished cut, set or class |
| Before/after | 0-1 | family/versus | the owner's two photos, same angle, labels "Before" "After" |
| Schedule / openings | 1 | family/list | type-only: up to 4 classes or slots with day and time |
| Offer / first visit | 1 | family/offer | price and terms in type |
| Tips or how-to | 1 | family/carousel-cover + inner + cta | 3-5 slides: aftercare, a stretch, a form cue |
| Client or member quote | 0-1 | family/quote | a review the owner supplied, first name |

## Rhythm
- Salons and spas: Tue to Thu 11:00am or 7:00pm local; openings Story the morning of.
- Gyms and studios: Sun 6:00pm schedule for the week; Mon 6:00am motivation-free class post; Fri 12pm weekend classes.
- "Openings today" Stories at 8:00am when a chair or a spot frees up.
- Stories: polls ("short or shoulder-length"), countdown to a workshop, a reshare of a client tag.
- Series ideas: "Chair of the week", "Sunday schedule", "Form check Friday", "Fresh fade Friday".

## Hooks that work
- "Two chairs open Thursday after 3."
- "This week at the studio: four classes, one new one."
- "First class is $10. Mats are on us."
- "Six weeks between these two photos. Same client, same light."
- "Book with Maya: link in bio, Tuesdays and Thursdays."
- "Your color lasts longer if you wait 72 hours to wash it."
- "Saturday 9am is the beginners class. Come as you are."
- "Fresh fade, Friday at 4:15."

## Style family
- Salons, spas, lash and nail studios: default `quiet_luxury`; `editorial` when the photos are bright and the shop is minimal.
- Gyms, bootcamps, trainers, dance: default `bold_type`; `clean_modern` for pilates and yoga studios with a calm brand.
- Palette from the real space: the chair leather, the wall colour, the logo, the sign. Blush, clay, warm stone and black for salons; chalk white, rubber black and one hard accent (signal orange, lime, red) for gyms. Never purple, violet, lavender or magenta, even for a spa.
- Imagery: the owner's photos only for work and people. A CC stand-in may show an empty studio, tools, towels, a mat. Never a stock model as "our client". Type-only for schedules and offers.

## Pitfalls
- A before/after built from stock or AI faces. Only the owner's photos, and state "client consent assumed; confirm before posting" in Assumptions.
- Body-shaming or weight language: "summer body", "bikini body", "lose the jiggle", "guilt-free", "earn your dinner", "no excuses", "beach ready".
- Promising results: "lose 10 lbs in 30 days", "guaranteed", "clear skin in a week".
- A "Book now" pill on the image. Booking is words: "Book at the link in bio", "Call [phone]".
- Inventing prices, stylist names, certifications or class times.
- Gym clichés: flexing silhouettes, flames, barbell clip art, "beast mode".
- A class schedule with no days or times on the image, or weekdays not computed for dated workshops.

## Defaults when the user gave nothing
- Platforms: Instagram feed + Story, Facebook same image. TikTok cover (`tiktok_cover`) only if they mention TikTok.
- Sizes: `ig_feed_portrait` 1080x1350, `ig_story` 1080x1920, story MP4.
- "A post": 1 feed + 1 Story + 1 Story MP4. "A week": 6 posts, 1 carousel, 3+ Stories, 1 Reel.
- Assumptions to state: booking method is [link in bio]; prices and class times as given, missing ones are [placeholders]; client consent for photos assumed and must be confirmed; no photos means type-only.
