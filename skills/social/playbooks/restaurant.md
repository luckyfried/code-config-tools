# Playbook: Restaurant
Restaurants, taquerias, food trucks, pizza, bars, breweries, diners, takeout counters.

## What actually works
- The recurring deal, named exactly how the owner names it: "Taco Tuesday", "Wing Wednesday", "Half-off Happy Hour 3-6". People plan their week around a name and a number.
- The number as the hero: "$2", "2 for $15", "50 cents a wing". Set huge, read in one second.
- The dish they actually serve, shot close and messy: cheese pull, the char on the crust, the salsa drip. Their photo beats any stock photo.
- The daily special as a quick Story: "Today: birria, until it's gone." Same-day, low effort, high return.
- The cook, the truck window, the regulars at the bar. Names only if given.
- For trucks: where you'll be. "Today 11-2 at [location]" is the whole post.

## Post mix
A week is 6 posts. A single "make my post" ask is one hero plus its Story.

| Post type | Share | Study (library examples, inspiration only) | Image shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recurring deal | 1-2 | bold_type/offer | type-only: the price huge, the day, short terms |
| Hero dish | 2 | bold_type/hero | their photo of the dish, close |
| Menu / specials list | 1 | bold_type/list | four items with prices, no photo |
| Behind the line | 1 | bold_type/carousel-cover + inner | press, grill, plating, the window |
| Event / game night / trivia | 0-1 | bold_type/event | date, time, place in type; their room if photographed |
| Regulars or staff | 0-1 | bold_type/quote | a line the owner supplied, first name |

## Rhythm
- Lunch spots: post 10:30-11:00am local, the day of. Dinner spots: 3:30-4:30pm.
- Recurring deal: the morning of the deal day (10:30am), plus a Story at 11:30am and 5pm.
- Night before: a short Story teaser for the deal ("Tomorrow. $2.").
- Fri 3pm: weekend specials or game-day post.
- Trucks: location Story every operating day by 9:30am, feed post Mon with the week's stops.
- Sold-out or "last 20 orders" Stories as they happen.
- Series ideas: the recurring deal itself, "Special of the day", "Staff pick Friday".

## Hooks that work
- "$2. All day. Every Tuesday."
- "$10 gets you five tacos and change for the jukebox."
- "Birria today until the pot's empty."
- "Wings are 75 cents until 6."
- "Truck's at [location] today, 11 to 2."
- "Tuesday has a plan now."
- "Al pastor or carnitas. Settle it in the comments."
- "Half-price pizza, Mondays, dine-in only."
- "We open at 11. The salsa verde is already gone by 1."

## Style family
- Default `bold_type`. Pick `playful_pop` for food trucks, casual counters, kid-friendly spots. Pick `editorial` for a wine bar, a tasting menu or a quiet bistro where the photos are good.
- Palette from the real thing: the sign, the logo, the dish, the place. A taqueria called El Sol: sun yellow, chile red, warm cream. A pizza place: tomato red, basil green, crust tan. Respect the cuisine and culture without costume. Never purple, never navy+teal+coral.
- Imagery: their photos first. A CC stand-in may show an ingredient or the general dish only if it matches the words exactly (tacos for tacos). A deal post can be type-only (a big price on a strong colour wins), but search anyway: an appetising stand-in of the exact dish makes a great Story, carousel or second image. If the sheet has nothing great, stay type-only.

## Pitfalls
- A stock burrito on a taco post. The picture must match the headline, or show no food at all.
- Sombreros, chili-pepper clip art, maracas, chef hats, "fiesta" fonts, flag bunting.
- Repeating the deal terms in every caption. State them once; other captions say "the $2 tacos".
- Inventing hours, an address, "since 1998", "award-winning", ingredients or a "secret recipe".
- An "Order now" or "See menu" pill on the image. CTA is words: "Tuesdays, all day", "link in bio".
- A whole menu as a wall of text. Four items on the image, the rest in the caption or a carousel.
- A date on an evergreen deal image. "Every Tuesday" goes on the image; the date goes in the plan.
- Alcohol deals aimed at people under 21, or "bottomless" promises the owner did not make.

## Defaults when the user gave nothing
- Platforms: Instagram feed + Story, Facebook gets the same 1080x1350 image.
- Sizes: `ig_feed_portrait` 1080x1350, `ig_story` 1080x1920, story MP4.
- "A post": 1 feed + 1 Story + 1 Story MP4. "A week": 6 posts, 1 carousel, 3+ Stories, 1 Reel.
- Post the next real deal day at 10:30am local (lunch) or 3:30pm (dinner), date computed.
- Assumptions to state: no address/hours given ([address], [hours] in caption); type-only because no photos; dine-in and takeout both assumed unless said; palette chosen for them from the name.
