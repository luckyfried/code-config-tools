# Playbook: Bakery and cafe
Bakeries, cafes, coffee shops, coffee carts, donut shops, ice cream, juice bars.

## What actually works
- The inside shot: a croissant torn open, a cinnamon roll pulled apart, a crema swirl from above. Texture sells, a whole loaf on a plate does not.
- Timestamps people can plan around: "Sourdough out of the oven at 7:10", "Kouign-amann gone by 9 most Saturdays". Only use times the owner gave.
- Sell-out posts. "Sold out of cardamom buns at 10:40" makes tomorrow's line. Post it the same day, in Stories.
- The person behind the counter, named only if the owner named them: hands shaping dough, the barista mid-pour, flour on an apron.
- The seasonal or one-day item with a clear window: "Pumpkin cruffins, Fri-Sun only".
- Preorders with a hard cutoff for weekends and holidays: "Order by Thu 5pm for Saturday pickup".

## Post mix
A week is 6 posts. A single "make my post" ask is one hero plus its Story.

| Post type | Share | Study (library examples, inspiration only) | Image shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero product (the inside shot) | 2 | warm_craft/hero | their photo of the item cut, torn or poured |
| Special or deal of the day | 1 | warm_craft/offer | type-only price card, or offer over a soft photo crop |
| Preorder / seasonal window | 1 | warm_craft/event | the item, with the order-by date and pickup day |
| Behind the scenes | 1 | warm_craft/story or carousel-cover | 3-5 slides: dough, oven, tray, counter |
| Menu or "what's in the case" | 1 | warm_craft/list | four items with prices, no photo |
| Regulars / staff / a quote | 0-1 | warm_craft/quote | a customer line the owner supplied, with first name |

## Rhythm
- Feed: Tue to Sat, 7:00-8:00am local, before the morning run. Sat is the strongest day.
- Thu 7:30am: weekend preorder post with the cutoff ("order by Fri noon").
- Fri and Sat 6:45am Story: "Out of the oven" with the first tray.
- Same day, whenever it happens: "Sold out of X" Story. Never a feed post.
- Sunday afternoon: a quiet BTS or "see you Tuesday" if closed Mondays.
- Series ideas: "First tray" (every Sat), "Case check" (Fri list of what's in), "Bake of the week".
- Stories carry daily news (sell-outs, polls like "rye or seeded"); feed carries the evergreen and the deals.

## Hooks that work
- "Out of the oven at 7:10. Gone by 9, usually."
- "Cardamom buns are back. Saturdays only."
- "Sold out of sourdough at 10:40. Tomorrow we're baking twelve more."
- "Order by Thursday 5pm. Pick up Saturday morning."
- "$3 drip and a day-old scone, every weekday till 9."
- "This is what 36 hours of fermentation looks like."
- "Pumpkin cruffins, Friday to Sunday, while the tray lasts."
- "The case at 7am. Pick one."

## Style family
- Default `warm_craft`. Pick `editorial` for a third-wave coffee shop, a minimal cafe, or when the photos are bright and airy. Pick `playful_pop` for coffee carts, donut and ice cream shops, kid-heavy crowds.
- Palette from the real thing: crust brown, butter yellow, flour cream, espresso, tomato red for a jam or berry accent, sage for a matcha place. Pull from the logo or awning if given. Never purple, even for ube or lavender items: show the item in its photo and keep the palette warm.
- Imagery: the owner's photos first, always. With none, a good CC photo of the exact bake the post names (a sourdough crumb shot, a croissant pulled apart, a cake slice) is a stand-in: use it on the hero/story/carousel posts and list those files in Assumptions as "swap in your own photo". Bread sells by its crumb and crust; a week with no bread in it fails. Keep price cards and lists type-only. Reject any photo that is dull, blurry or clearly a supermarket product.

## Pitfalls
- A stock baker or storefront presented as theirs. A CC bake is a stand-in and says so in Assumptions.
- Seven text cards in a row on the same paper: no rhythm, nothing to look at.
- Coffee-bean borders, steam swirl clip art, chef hats, rolling pins as decoration.
- Inventing bake times, sell-out times, "family recipe since 1987" or "best in town".
- "Indulge", "treat yourself", "mouthwatering", "delectable", "freshly baked goodness".
- Repeating the price and hours in every caption. State the deal once.
- Renaming their thing. If they say "Croissant Friday", it is "Croissant Friday" everywhere, same spelling and caps.
- A menu as a wall of text: max four items on a list image; the rest goes in the caption.
- A preorder post with no cutoff day and time, or a weekday not computed by command.

## Defaults when the user gave nothing
- Platforms: Instagram feed + Story, Facebook gets the same 1080x1350 image.
- Sizes: `ig_feed_portrait` 1080x1350, `ig_story` 1080x1920, story MP4.
- "A post": 1 feed + 1 Story + 1 Story MP4. "A week": 6 posts, 1 carousel, 3+ Stories, 1 Reel.
- Post at 7:30am local on the next real day the item is sold (compute the date).
- Assumptions to state: no address or hours given (caption uses [address] / [hours]); photos are stand-ins or type-only; palette chosen for them; the pickup/preorder method is [link in bio] until they confirm.
