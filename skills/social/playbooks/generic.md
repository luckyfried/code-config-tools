# Playbook: Generic
Anything the other playbooks don't cover: trades (plumbers, roofers, electricians), auto shops, pet groomers, tutors, photographers, florists, cleaners, local services, or a business type you can't tell.

## First: borrow the nearest playbook
Ask what the business sells and how people buy it, then take the closest playbook's post mix and pitfalls as a second reference:
- Sells something you eat or drink: `restaurant.md` or `bakery-cafe.md`.
- Sells appointments with a person (groomer, tutor, photographer, cleaner, mechanic): `salon-studio-fitness.md`.
- Sells a physical item shipped or picked up (florist, print shop): `etsy-ecommerce.md`.
- Sells expertise to other businesses: `coach-freelancer-b2b.md`.
- The ask is about a date: add `events.md`. About something new: add `product-launch.md`.
Write which one you borrowed and why in `## Scope`.

## What actually works
- The finished job in a real photo: the clean drain, the groomed dog, the repaired bumper, the bouquet on a counter.
- Before/after from their own photos (a before/after design), when the work changes how something looks.
- The price or the availability as a plain fact: "Gutter cleaning from $129", "Two openings next week".
- The service area, named exactly: "Serving Midtown and East Sac". Only places they gave.
- A useful tip from the trade: "Turn the water off here before you call anyone."
- The owner or crew on the job, with names if given.

## Post mix
A week is 5 posts. A single "make my post" ask is one hero plus its Story.

| Post type | Share | Study (library examples, inspiration only) | Image shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| The work | 2 | clean_modern/hero | their photo of a finished job |
| Before/after | 0-1 | clean_modern/versus | their two photos, same angle |
| Offer / availability | 1 | clean_modern/offer | type-only price or openings |
| Tips | 1 | clean_modern/list or carousel | 4 tips from the trade, type-only |
| Review / quote | 0-1 | clean_modern/quote | a review they pasted, first name |

## Rhythm
- Feed: Tue and Thu 7:00am or 6:00pm local; Sat 9:00am for home services.
- Openings or "available tomorrow" as Stories, the morning of.
- Seasonal: post the seasonal service 2-3 weeks before the season (gutters in October, AC tune-ups in April).
- Series ideas: "Job of the week", "Quick fix Friday", "Ask us anything" Story question sticker.

## Hooks that work
- "Two openings next Tuesday. Mornings only."
- "Gutter cleaning from $129, single-story homes."
- "Same driveway, three hours apart."
- "Turn this valve off before you call a plumber."
- "Serving Midtown and East Sac, six days a week."
- "Hank went in shaggy and came out a schnauzer."
- "Booked through November 20. Taking December now."

## Style family
- Default `clean_modern` when unsure; it suits almost anything. Pick `bold_type` for trades and deals, `warm_craft` for florists and pet services, `editorial` for photographers, `quiet_luxury` for premium services.
- Palette from the logo, the van, the uniform, the storefront. Otherwise warm white, charcoal and one confident accent (safety orange, forest, brick, sky blue). Never purple, never navy+teal+coral.
- Imagery: their photos first. A CC stand-in may show tools, materials or a generic place, labelled in Assumptions. No good photo: type-only.

## Pitfalls
- Trade clip art: wrenches crossed, cartoon plumbers, house icons, sparkles on "clean".
- A stock "happy customer" or "our team" photo.
- Inventing licences, insurance, years in business, service areas, guarantees or prices.
- "Book now" or "Call now" pills on the image. Words only: "Call [phone]", "link in bio".
- Seven near-identical "we do X" posts. Mix work, offer, tip and review.
- Using a playbook rule that doesn't fit (a bakery's sell-out post for a plumber). Borrow only what transfers.

## Defaults when the user gave nothing
- Platforms: Instagram feed + Story, Facebook same image (Facebook and Nextdoor-style local reach matter for services).
- Sizes: `ig_feed_portrait` 1080x1350, `ig_story` 1080x1920, story MP4.
- "A post": 1 feed + 1 Story + 1 Story MP4. "A week": 5 posts, 1 carousel, 2+ Stories.
- Assumptions to state: which playbook was borrowed; contact is [phone] / [link in bio]; service area and prices only as given; photos theirs or type-only; palette chosen for them.
