# The styles

Each style is one card, `styles/<id>.json`: its two faces, its palette rule, the moves and transitions it may use, its
easing, its finish, its music recipe, and its closer. A person names a look in their own words; find the words below
and use the style's id. A board is the person's story told in one of these styles.

| id | in one sentence, for the person | what a person might call it |
|---|---|---|
| editorial-quiet | Your photos, softened a touch, set in a serif with wide white space around them, like a quiet magazine spread. | editorial, magazine, minimal, calm, clean and elegant, Kinfolk, serif, airy, understated, slow |
| kinetic-type | Big bold words that slam in on the beat over two flat colors, cut hard against your photos full bleed. | kinetic typography, bold type, punchy, loud, hype, poster type, typographic, energetic, motion type |
| scrapbook | Your photos dropped onto paper like polaroids, taped down and doodled on, finished with a hand-lettered cover. | scrapbook, collage, polaroid, handmade, crafty, journal, cute, washi tape, photo dump |
| analog-home-movie | Warm grainy film with a flickering frame, a date stamp in the corner, and light leaking in, like a home movie. | home movie, super 8, film, vintage, retro, VHS, camcorder, nostalgic, old film, 8mm, lo-fi |
| swiss-grid | Clean black and white on a strict grid with one accent color, every photo snapping into its own cell. | swiss, grid, modernist, international style, minimal poster, Bauhaus, structured, design-y |
| cinematic-letterbox | Black bars, slow camera moves, and a film's color grade, with your words set as a movie title. | cinematic, movie, film trailer, letterbox, widescreen, epic, dramatic, blockbuster |
