# Font licenses

The kit sets type only in the faces its style cards name, kept under `styles/<style>/` beside each style's `OFL.txt`.
Every face is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL), from the Google Fonts repository
(github.com/google/fonts, `ofl/`), free for commercial use. Local `.ttf` files only; nothing loads from a CDN.
