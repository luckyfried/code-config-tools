`make_person.py` sets these photographs in place of drawings for every post, clip and post of dani's that names one
in its own row; every caption on one of them is written from it. Each is CC0 or public domain, copied from another
kit's fixture in this repository or from the Code Config demo seeds' media (`scripts/demos/seeds/media` in the
configurator repository), at 1350 px on the long side at most. Nothing here was downloaded for this kit.
